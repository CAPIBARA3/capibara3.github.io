
> [!ABSTRACT] Summary
> This page explains how to join specifically the GAMMA Team within the CAPIBARA Collaboration. If you want to join the CAPIBARA Collaboration, but not specifically the GAMMA Team, please refer to [00 Applying to Join](01_applying_to_join.md).

# Requirements

The same requirements apply as in the general application form, the GAMMA Team has not further requirements.

# Steps

If you want to apply visit the our [GAMMA application form](https://capibara3.github.io/gamma/apply.html) and fill in required information. While we have listed some information on ongoing work and topics, please do apply if you have any other idea, we want to hear you!

The procedure regarding the signed copy of the membership agreement and other information during and after the application is the same as in the general application. The same collaboration-wide privacy policy applies.

Please, do read the important documentation recommended in the general page.

> [!SUCCESS]
> Welcome to the team! Here are your next steps:
> - Get in touch with the project lead writing to gamma.capibara [at] outlook [dot] com, regarding your role/tasks/focus.
> - Get started with the [tech basics](1_tech_foundations).
> - Familiarize with ongoing work and structure of the team/project and our repositories.
