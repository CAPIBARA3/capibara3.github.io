# Call for New Members (2025)

> [!CAUTION] Call Closed
> **This recruitment call is no longer active.** Please view our latest active call at [[updates/call-for-new-members-2026|Call for New Members 2026]].

After one year of development and planning, we are thrilled to announce a call for new collaboration members. This is a volunteer opportunity open to students of all levels (high school to PhD) and fields—not just STEM, but also outreach, art, and social media.

---

## Requirements

* **English Communication:** Strong oral and written communication skills for collaboration.
* **Student Status:** Open to all levels (High School through PhD). CAPIBARA is fully student-run.
* **Curiosity & Passion:** No prior experience required—just a willingness to learn and contribute.
* **Time Commitment:** Typically 1–6 hours/week at your own pace.

---

## Steps to Apply

1. [[contact|Get in Touch]] expressing your interest with a brief background intro.
2. Receive an email with onboard access to our private GitHub repositories and meeting schedules.
3. Join working groups and begin collaborating at your own pace!
