# Year 1 Collaboration Report Published

> **Author:** Joan Alcaide  
> **Date:** June 15, 2023  
> **Category:** Mission Update  

The CAPIBARA team is excited to announce the publication of its **Year 1 Status Report**, covering our progress, hardware initiatives, and future mission roadmap:

* **First Year Milestones:** How we put an idea onto paper while building valuable institutional partnerships.
* **CAPICR:** Next steps toward bringing our cosmic ray detector concept to reality.
* **CAPIGX:** Our long-term vision for a high-energy monitoring mission.
