# Exploring the High-Energy Universe

> [!INFO]
> CAPIBARA is an international student collaboration learning about gamma-ray bursts and cosmic rays as well as designing and building instruments to detect those. [[join-us|Join Us!]]

# Two Missions, One Vision

## CRD – Cosmic Ray Detector
A student-built instrument integrated into the OBA FARADAY CubeSat. FLying in LEO to detect solar and extra-solar primary cosmic rays, using Time-of-Flight techniques to distinguish their origins.

[[index|Learn about CRD]]

## GAMMA – Gamma-ray Analysis for Mutli-Messenger Astrophysics

A mission concept to observe gamma-ray bursts and advancing in our understanding of fundmental physics.

[[GAMMA/index|Learn about GAMMA]]
